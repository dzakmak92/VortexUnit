1. Controller

No data protection officer has been appointed, as the legal requirements for one are not met. For any privacy questions, please write to the address above.

2. Principles

We process personal data only on the basis of the applicable law (GDPR, Austrian Data Protection Act DSG, Austrian Telecommunications Act TKG 2021). This website is deliberately built to collect as little data as possible: there are no analytics or tracking services, no advertising and no embedded third-party content (fonts, images and animations are served from our own server).

3. Hosting & server log files

This website is hosted by Vercel Inc., 440 N Barranca Ave #4133, Covina, CA 91723, USA. Vercel delivers the site through a worldwide server network, which may involve transferring data to the USA. Vercel is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, for which the European Commission has issued an adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). Vercel’s data processing agreement, including the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, also applies (Art. 28, Art. 46 GDPR).

When you visit the website, Vercel processes technically necessary data in server log files:

  • IP address
  • date and time of access
  • page or file requested
  • amount of data transferred and HTTP status code
  • browser type and operating system
  • referrer URL (the page visited before)

This data is needed to deliver the website securely and reliably (e.g. to fend off attacks). We do not combine it with other data. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in secure operation (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

4. TLS encryption

This website is only transmitted in encrypted form (HTTPS). You can tell by the “https://” and the padlock symbol in your browser’s address bar.

5. No cookies, no tracking

This website sets no cookies and stores nothing else on your device either (no localStorage, no fingerprinting). No data is sent to analytics or advertising services when you visit. A cookie banner is therefore not needed.

6. Contacting us

If you write to us via the contact form or by email, we process the details you provide (name, email address, message and whether you are enquiring as a business or a creator) in order to answer your enquiry.

Important: the contact form does not send any data to our server. When you submit it, your own email app opens with a prepared message; you send it yourself through your email provider.

For enquiries about a possible collaboration, the legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures); otherwise it is our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). We do not pass your details on to third parties without your consent.

7. Working together & projects

If we work together, we process the data needed for the quote, contract, delivery and invoicing (e.g. contact details, billing details, project communication) on the basis of Art. 6 (1) (b) and (c) GDPR. Where we process personal data on behalf of our clients as part of a project, we conclude a data processing agreement for it (Art. 28 GDPR).

This website itself involves no registration, no login and no payment.

8. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purpose concerned. Enquiries that do not lead to a collaboration are deleted after three years at the latest. Contract and invoice data are kept for seven years in line with the statutory retention obligation (§ 132 Austrian Federal Fiscal Code, BAO). Server log files are deleted automatically by the host after a short time.

9. Your rights

You generally have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). You can withdraw any consent you have given at any time with effect for the future. Simply write to us at contact@vortexunit.de.

Right to lodge a complaint

If you believe that the processing of your data breaches data protection law, you can lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority. In Austria this is the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde), Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, dsb.gv.at.
